## 👋 About Me

I'm **Ademola Bhadmus**, a Senior Quality Assurance Engineer, Software Development Engineer in Test (SDET), and Quality Engineering professional with **10+ years of experience** designing, implementing and improving software testing strategies across diverse technology environments.

My experience spans **web, mobile, API and performance test automation**, with a strong focus on building scalable automation frameworks, embedding quality into software delivery pipelines, and helping engineering teams deliver reliable products.

I believe quality engineering goes beyond finding bugs. It is about building the right processes, tools, engineering culture and technical capabilities that prevent defects and make software delivery predictable.

Throughout my career, I have:

- Led and mentored QA teams across multiple organisations.
- Designed and implemented automation frameworks from the ground up.
- Championed Behaviour-Driven Development (BDD) and shift-left testing.
- Integrated automated testing into CI/CD pipelines.
- Built developer tools that simplify and accelerate testing workflows.
- Introduced AI-assisted development practices into test automation.
- Trained QA engineers and software developers in automation best practices.

Currently, I work as a **QA Automation Engineer at Northumbria Healthcare NHS Foundation Trust**, contributing to quality engineering initiatives using Playwright, .NET and Reqnroll.

I'm particularly interested in the intersection of **Quality Engineering, AI, agentic automation and developer productivity**.

## 🧠 My Quality Engineering Philosophy

I approach software quality as an engineering responsibility shared across the entire delivery lifecycle.

My principles:

1. **Quality starts with understanding:** Build a clear understanding of business requirements, acceptance criteria and user expectations.
2. **Shift quality left:** Identify risks and prevent defects as early as possible in the development lifecycle.
3. **Automate strategically:** Automation should solve real problems, improve feedback loops and provide maintainable value.
4. **Think beyond the happy path:** Explore edge cases, negative scenarios, integration risks and unexpected user behaviour.
5. **Measure what matters:** Use meaningful testing metrics and feedback to improve engineering decisions.
6. **Share knowledge:** Build teams where quality engineering skills are continuously developed and shared.
7. **Embrace innovation responsibly:** Use AI and emerging technologies to enhance engineering productivity without compromising test reliability or accountability.
